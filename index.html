THE WERATREAN ARCHIVE

An archive for better sessions.

The Weratrean Archive creates system-agnostic resources for tabletop RPG storytellers: encounter ideas, tables, prompts, reference sheets and practical tools designed to be useful when you actually need them.

The Archivist catalogues what finds its way here from countless worlds: places, people, encounters, and fragments of events that may have happened somewhere — or may yet happen. Each Archive Record is prepared for use at your table.
