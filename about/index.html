ABOUT THE ARCHIVIST

Good tools disappear into good games.

The Dungeon Archivist is a growing collection of practical, system-neutral resources for Game Masters and tabletop RPG storytellers.

The idea behind the Archive is simple: useful RPG tools should be quick to understand, easy to use at the table and worth keeping within reach.

Instead of trying to replace a Game Master's creativity, the Archive is designed to support it — with tables, prompts, generators, reference material and small tools that can turn an empty page into a playable idea.

New resources will be added gradually. The emphasis is on quality, clarity and practical usefulness rather than producing as much material as possible.

The archive is only beginning.
