ABOUT THE ARCHIVIST

Some worlds survive only in the Archive.

The Weratrean Archive is a collection of practical, system-agnostic resources for Game Masters and tabletop RPG storytellers.

Records of places that were, may yet be, and never were.

A journey ends; a notebook finds its way here. The Archivist makes room for it. There are accounts of the same city under different skies, and letters addressed to streets no map remembers.

Two travellers gave me different names for the same door. I have filed both. Neither would tell me what was on the other side.

— The Archivist

At your table, these records become encounter ideas, characters, locations, tables, and adventures. Use them as written or change them to fit your game. Each resource includes the practical details you need to get started.
